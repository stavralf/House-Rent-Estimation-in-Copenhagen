# House-Rent-Estimation-in-Copenhagen

## About
We collect information from a popular Housing Portal in Copenhagen. We then build a linear regression model which incorporates various apartment characteristics and finally provides an estimation of the expected monthly net rent. Variables selected for the prediction task are: Size, Neighborhood, Number of Rooms & Balcony. Given that, our model is able to provide an estimation for the expected net rent while including the majority of the advertised apartments for rent in Copenhagen.

## Overview
Variables are explored and new variables are crafted before fitted to multi-linear regression models and then further examined and assessed. Inference and prediction accuracy of the models are discussed from various perspectives, while techniques such as Weighted Least Squares(WLS) for tackling heteroskedasticity of errors or Ridge Regression to robust prediction efficiency and mitigate collinearity and multicollinearity are employed. 

## Future Development
An interactive platform where users looking for renting a house state their preferences and obtain predictions coupled with confidence intervals on the Monthly Net Rent value.
